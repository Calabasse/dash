Peptide Dosing + Clearance Estimator

Build a peptide stack and estimate active-load decay from each compound's own half-life. Nothing is uploaded: your data stays in your browser and in a plain-text file you save to your own device. Estimates only — not medical advice.
Read this first. These compounds are NOT a uniform category. Some (tesamorelin) are FDA-approved prescription drugs for one narrow indication. Some (sermorelin) were once FDA-approved and later withdrawn. Most (BPC-157, TB-500, CJC-1295, ipamorelin, melanotan II, AOD-9604) are unapproved research chemicals with no completed human trials — for several, the FDA has taken explicit action (BPC-157: determined unsafe for compounding). Check the Status column in the Compound Library below before assuming anything here is a legitimate, regulated medicine.
Known gap: semaglutide, tirzepatide, and other FDA-approved GLP-1 weight-loss drugs are NOT on this page — no research monograph for them exists yet in the source project (ped/corpus/monographs/). This page only covers peptides already researched there, flagged openly rather than silently omitted.

Estimated Active Load

Each administration contributes a decay curve from that compound's own half-life; the plot sums them (superposition). "Active load" is a relative mg estimate, not a measured serum concentration — no established serum assay/calibration exists for most of these compounds, unlike testosterone's ng/dL. Where a compound's half-life is estimated or low-confidence (see Compound Library), the whole curve inherits that uncertainty.
