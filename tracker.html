Why this weekly amount gives this steady-state peak
Modeled drug amount under an effective apparent-decay model, in mg-equivalent. Not a serum concentration and not a dosing recommendation. Testosterone cypionate is released slowly from an oil depot, so real serum curves rise and fall differently. Equal mg across compounds does not mean equal effect or safety. In instant-input mode each dose enters the body at once and decays with k = ln 2 / half-life; depot mode adds first-order absorption from the injection site at rate ka; steady-state and load (L) figures are mathematical equivalents under that model, not body burden or clinical targets. Scenario D is a theoretical initial condition, not a dosing recommendation.
